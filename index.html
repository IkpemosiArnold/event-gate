The rules it applies

  • A browser event and a server event are one conversion when event_name and event_id match and the second arrives within 48 hours of the first. After that, Meta counts both.
  • For website events, event_time is at most 7 days old. One older event and Meta rejects the whole request, so it is held here instead.
  • Email, phone, name, city, state, zip, country, birth date, gender and external_id are SHA-256 hashed. Email, phone and name are normalised first and tested against Meta's own worked examples. IP, user agent, fbp and fbc are sent as they are.
  • URLs keep the domain and a short list of campaign params. A path or param with a health term goes, and so does every custom field not on the list.

Times are read against a sample clock, 12 Nov 2026 at 09:00 GMT. The health term list is a short sample. It catches the names it knows and misses new brands and misspellings.
